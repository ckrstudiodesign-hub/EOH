Founder & Chief Empowerment Officer

Dr. Shaheena Kadri

Physician · ICF Certified Life Coach · Peak Performance & Empowerment Coach · Author · International Speaker

Her journey began in medicine, with nearly two decades of clinical practice across India and the UAE. As a homeopathic physician she treated the body, but she saw something deeper: people whose gifts lay buried under self-doubt, fear and unfulfilled aspiration.

She expanded into human behaviour, psychology and coaching science, becoming an ICF Certified Life Coach, Peak Performance and Empowerment Coach, Mindfulness Trainer, Leadership Facilitator and published author. Emerge on Horizon grew from that work.
